Who We Are

Soul Doctor Movement (Salvation Family) is a faith-based global movement called to build the temple of the Lord in hearts, homes, and territories by gathering, healing, equipping, and establishing God’s people as living temples.

What We Do

We create prayer altars, discipleship spaces, teachings, resources, and expressions of outreach and media that restore foundations, align lives to heaven’s blueprint, and raise kingdom builders for generational impact in homes, communities, and nations.

How to Join

You can join the movement by connecting with Salvation Family, committing to the daily prayer altar, plugging into discipleship, and serving with your gifts as a builder in your home, city, and nation.
